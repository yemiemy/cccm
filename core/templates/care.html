Daycare, After-School and Summer Camp

Our daycare service is for 3 months to 4-year-old children. Our community centre has worked to incorporate early learning for every child along with provincial pedagogy for early learning to strengthen the quality of our program and ensure a positive experience that promotes the health, safety, nutrition, and wellbeing of your children.

We also provide after school care for school-aged children from age 4 to 12. We have professional early childhood educators/ Volunteers available that will assist your children with their homework.
